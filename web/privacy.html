Privacy

Fuelish is a private, invite-only app run by one family for itself. It isn't sold, and nothing in it is shared with anyone outside the people invited.

What it keeps

From Google sign-in: your name, email address, profile picture and Google's account id, so you can sign in. Nothing else from your Google account is read.

From the recorder: the drives you record: GPS location, phone motion sensors, and engine data from an OBD adapter, plus basic details of the phone and app.

Who can see it

People with an account on this Fuelish can see each other's trips. A single trip can be shared by link, which shows that trip to anyone who has the link until it's turned off.

Where it's kept

On Cloudflare (Workers, D1 and R2). It's kept until the owner deletes it.

Leaving

Ask the owner to remove your account and your trips.
